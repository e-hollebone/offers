# Vendor invoice approval

*A worked example of the 90-second format. The details are illustrative; replace them with your own.*

## Question
Can I approve this vendor invoice?

## Answer
Yes, if three things are true: it matches a purchase order, the amount is within your approval limit, and the work or goods were received. If any one is missing, do not approve it.

## Next step
Approve it in [Accounting System Name]. Payment runs on the normal cycle. If one check fails, reply to the sender with what is missing and copy [Finance Contact Name].

## If it does not fit
No purchase order, or the amount is above your limit: send it to [Finance Contact Name] the same day. Do not hold it; late invoices cost time later.
