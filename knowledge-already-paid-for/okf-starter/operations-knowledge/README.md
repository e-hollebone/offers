# Operations Knowledge

**This file is for people.** If you are an agent, read `index.md` instead.

## Why this folder exists

This is where [Company Name] keeps what it knows about how operations runs: procedures, decisions, checklists and the answers to questions people keep asking. It belongs to the company, not to any one person, so it stays useful when people change roles or leave.

## What belongs here

- How we do things: procedures and checklists
- Why we decided things: short decision notes
- Who owns what, and when it was last checked

## What does not belong here

- Passwords, keys or tokens (keep those in your password manager and refer to the item name)
- Personal data about customers or staff
- Drafts nobody owns

## How to use it

1. Look in `index.md` for the list. Each line tells you what a file is, who owns it, and when it was last reviewed.
2. Open the one file you need. You do not have to read the whole folder.
3. If something is wrong or out of date, tell the owner named in the file.

## How to add something

1. Copy `procedures/TEMPLATE_90-second-sop.md`.
2. Fill in the question, the answer, the next step, the owner and the review date.
3. Add one line for it in `index.md`.
